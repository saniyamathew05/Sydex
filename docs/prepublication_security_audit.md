# Horizon pre-publication security audit

Audit date: 6 November 2026  
Scope: Flutter client, checked-in Supabase migrations, and the connected Horizon Supabase project. This is an engineering audit, not legal advice or a compliance certification.

## Implemented safeguards

| Finding | Severity | Status | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Repeated payment submissions could create more than one payment after a timeout or double tap. | Critical | Fixed | `record_payment_idempotent` uses a company-scoped unique idempotency key. The payment screen retains that key and signature path until the server confirms success. |
| No database-owned record of financial creates, edits, or deletes. | High | Fixed | `financial_audit_log` is written by database triggers. RLS gives owners read-only access to their active company events. |
| Ledger import batches were visible to any owner, including owners of another company. | Critical | Fixed | Import-batch read policy now also requires the active company ID. |
| Payments can still be edited or deleted through legacy owner workflows. | High | Open | Existing functions preserve an audit entry after the new trigger, but a reversal/correction workflow must replace mutation and deletion before broad release. |
| Salesman customer and transaction policies require route-assignment tests with two independent business accounts. | High | Open | Current policies are company-scoped. Tightening them without real role-account tests risks interrupting field workflows. |
| Signatures are stored in Supabase Storage and downloaded through the authenticated client. | Medium | Needs configuration verification | Confirm the `payment-signatures` bucket is private and its object policies restrict each company before release. |
| Privacy notices, account deletion, and legal document acceptance are incomplete. | High | Open | These require operator contact details, retention decisions, and legal review; do not publish until supplied and implemented. |
| Backup, restore, and storage recovery procedures are not verified. | High | Open | Confirm Supabase plan backups, storage backup process, recovery owner, RPO, RTO, and restore test record. |

## Financial integrity rules now enforced

- Monetary values continue to use PostgreSQL `numeric` columns.
- The payment RPC locks the customer row while calculating and writing the payment.
- One idempotency key can yield only one payment per company.
- Audit timestamps and actor identities are generated by PostgreSQL, not accepted from Flutter.
- Audit records cannot be inserted, edited, or removed by client roles.

## Required release gates

1. Replace legacy payment update/delete actions with an explicit correction/reversal workflow.
2. Run cross-company and owner/salesman RLS tests with separate nonproduction test accounts.
3. Verify private Storage bucket policies for payment signatures and any attachments.
4. Configure and test database and Storage backup recovery.
5. Publish lawyer-reviewed privacy policy, terms, business agreement, retention schedule, and contact details.
6. Complete Google Play and Apple App Store privacy, account-deletion, and permission declarations.

## Tests executed

- `flutter test test/widget_test.dart` — passed.
- `flutter analyze lib/services/payment_service.dart` — passed.
- Connected Supabase schema verification — confirmed the protected idempotent RPC is executable only by `authenticated`/service database roles and has a locked search path.

## Findings still requiring external access or business decisions

- Google/Apple console publication settings.
- Supabase backup-plan and restore-test configuration.
- Privacy policy operator identity, privacy contact, retention periods, and jurisdiction-specific legal review.
- Two-account RLS validation using safe nonproduction data.
